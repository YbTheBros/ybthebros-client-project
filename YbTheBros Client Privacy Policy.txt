Privacy Policy and YbTheBros Client Policy

1. INTRODUCTION

This Privacy Policy and YbTheBros Client Policy explains how the YbTheBros Client team (“YbTheBros,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in connection with YbTheBros Client and related YbTheBros products.

By downloading, installing, accessing, or using YbTheBros Client, you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, do not use YbTheBros Client.

2. INFORMATION AND REPORTS

YbTheBros Client does not automatically collect personal information, device information, usage information, analytics, or telemetry from users.

We only receive information that users voluntarily provide when they submit a bug report, crash report, support request, feedback, or another report to the YbTheBros Client team.

A report may contain information that the user chooses to provide, such as screenshots, logs, error messages, YbTheBros Client version, Minecraft version, operating system, Java version, or other technical details needed to understand and fix the reported issue.

YbTheBros does not automatically collect this information. It is only received when a user voluntarily sends a report.

3. BUG REPORTING

If you find a bug, error, crash, exploit, compatibility problem, or another issue in YbTheBros Client, please report it to the YbTheBros Client team.

When possible, include useful details such as what happened, what you expected to happen, how to reproduce the problem, your YbTheBros Client version, your Minecraft version, and relevant screenshots, logs, or error messages.

Do not publicly publish or distribute private YbTheBros source code, protected files, credentials, security-sensitive information, or other confidential YbTheBros materials when reporting a bug.

You can message the YbTheBros Client team owner on Discord:
Discord: the_real_ybthebros

4. LOCAL DATA

YbTheBros Client may store settings, preferences, module configurations, key bindings, or similar configuration information locally on your device.

Information that remains only on your device and is not sent to the YbTheBros Client team is not collected by us.

5. USE OF VOLUNTARILY SUBMITTED REPORTS

Information voluntarily provided in reports may be used to investigate and fix bugs, crashes, and compatibility problems, provide support, improve YbTheBros Client, understand reported security or stability problems, and respond to the person who submitted the report.

We do not sell personal information.

6. REPORT RETENTION

Reports may be kept for as long as reasonably necessary to investigate the reported issue, provide support, maintain relevant records, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

Users should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive personal information in reports.

7. SHARING

Information voluntarily submitted in reports is not intended to be sold or shared for advertising.

Information may be disclosed where reasonably necessary to investigate a report, protect YbTheBros or its users, comply with applicable law, or use a service that is necessary to receive or process the report.

8. SECURITY

We take reasonable steps intended to protect information voluntarily submitted to the YbTheBros Client team.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should not include passwords, payment-card details, private documents, or other unnecessary sensitive information in reports.
